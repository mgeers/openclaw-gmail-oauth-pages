Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-27

This application is a personal automation tool operated solely by Martijn Geers for his own use. It accesses only his own Google account data (Gmail, Calendar, and related Google Workspace services) to power personal automations such as email triage and calendar checks.

Data accessed via Google APIs is processed locally on infrastructure controlled by the account owner and is never shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party. No data is used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.

This is not a public-facing product; there are no other users.
